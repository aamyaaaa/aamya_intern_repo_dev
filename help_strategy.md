# Help Strategy Reflection

## When do I prefer using AI vs Google?

I prefer using AI (like ChatGPT) when:
- I need an explanation in simple terms
- I want help understanding why something works
- I need examples or code snippets adapted to my own code
- I want faster feedback instead of searching multiple pages

I prefer Google when:
- I am looking for documentation, package references, or official guides
- I want multiple opinions from different sources (StackOverflow, blogs)
- I need the latest updates or version-specific information

AI helps me learn quickly, while Google helps me verify the most accurate or official information.

## When do I ask a colleague instead?
I ask a colleague when:
- I am blocked for too long and progress has stopped
- The issue involves organization-specific code or systems AI cannot access
- The problem affects other team members or requires coordination
- I need help understanding why something is done a certain way in the team

Colleagues provide important context that AI and Google cannot.


## What challenges do developers face when troubleshooting alone?
- It is easy to waste time searching without knowing the right keywords
- Bugs can seem simple but have hidden causes in other parts of the system
- Without feedback, it is hard to know if you are moving in the right direction
- Frustration can increase and slow down productivity

A good strategy balances self-learning and collaboration.


## Decision-Making Framework (Flowchart Included in Repo)

I created a flowchart in Miro that helps me decide whether to use:
- Google
- AI tools
- A colleague

It considers:
- Complexity of the issue
- Urgency of the work
- Sensitivity or privacy of data
- Whether the problem is personal learning or team related

## The gist of the flow chart is: 
Stuck on a problem? 

↓

Is it urgent or blocking others? → Ask colleague

↓

Is it about understanding a concept? → Ask AI

↓

Is it technical reference info? → Google search

↓

Still stuck after 20–30 min? → Ask colleague


## Summary

AI helps me understand and experiment.  
Google helps me research and validate.  
Colleagues help me collaborate and unblock team-specific issues.

Using the right tool at the right time keeps development efficient and reduces frustration.
